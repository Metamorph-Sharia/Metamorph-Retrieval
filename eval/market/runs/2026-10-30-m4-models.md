# Choosing the embedding model and its floor (M4, 2026-10-30)

How the market index's model and similarity floor were chosen. Everything ran on the platform's data published 2026-10-29: 7,022 index units (1,943 report passages, 3,685 release passages, 1,390 dataset cards, 4 industry cards; no sectors are defined yet).

## The candidates

| Version | What | Built in |
|---|---|---|
| `market-2026-10-30.4` | Full text only (PostgreSQL `indonesian`): the baseline | 1 s |
| `market-2026-10-30.5` | + `intfloat/multilingual-e5-base` (768 dimensions) | about 1 min |
| `market-2026-10-30.3` | + `BAAI/bge-m3` (1,024 dimensions) | about 4 min |

Both models are local and trained on Indonesian. They ran on an Apple M5 with sentence-transformers, at most 512 tokens per passage.

## Similarity: why bge-m3, and why 0.45

For each search case, the similarity of the first expected passage found by meaning, against the top similarity for ten questions the market can't answer (recipes, football, flights, weather, a phone camera, the flu…):

| | Expected passages | Their rank | Off-topic questions' best match |
|---|---|---|---|
| e5 | 0.80–0.91 | 1 to 49; one not in the first 50 | 0.74–0.83 |
| bge-m3 | 0.59–0.76, one at 0.44 | 1 to 8, one at 21; one not in the first 50 | 0.35–0.49 |

- **e5 crowds every similarity into 0.74–0.91**, so no floor separates relevant from off-topic. It also ranks the expected passages lower. It is not used.
- **bge-m3 spreads them.**
  - A floor of **0.45** keeps every expected passage but one.
  - It also keeps the one English dataset question whose table scores 0.45 ("sharia commercial bank total assets"; dataset cards are short, so their similarities run lower).
  - The cost is that two off-topic questions out of ten still get a match by meaning.
  - A floor of 0.50 drops all of them, and loses that dataset question. The question set is the gate, so 0.45 was chosen.

## Half the question's words: the floor alone didn't work

The words ranking joins the question's stems with OR. So **every off-topic question matched something by words**: "how to bake sourdough bread" shares "to" with some passage. No question ever got `no_match`, whatever the floor.

A match by words now has to hold at least half of the question's stems.

| Floor | Off-topic questions answered `no_match` |
|---|---|
| none, before the rule | 0 of 10 |
| 0.50, with the rule | 6 of 10 |
| 0.45, with the rule | 5 of 10 |

The rest match on common words. "Symptoms of the flu" holds "of" and "the". "Harga tiket pesawat ke Bali bulan depan" holds "harga", "bulan" and "depan". With bge-m3 the rule also *raised* search: 17 to 18 of 19, since fewer stray word matches dilute the fusion.

## Latency

`search_documents` through the gateway over HTTP, 23 questions:
- **first time:** p50 120 ms, p95 174 ms;
- **asked again:** p50 117 ms, p95 143 ms. The query's embedding is cached in process.

Exact vector search over 7,022 units needs no approximate index at this size.
